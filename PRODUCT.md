## Platform

web

## Users

Businesses in UK food production and retail that need a dependable workforce. The audience named in the brief is: operations directors, HR managers, production managers, retail operators and procurement decision-makers. No single primary user has been chosen; the owner confirmed the brief's audience list as the authority (2026-10-31).

Their job when they reach the site: judge whether Indo understands their operation well enough to be trusted with sourcing and managing the people it depends on, then make an enquiry.

## Product Purpose

Indo is a UK Human Resources and workforce management company. It procures, supplies and manages labour for businesses in the food and retail industries.

The website exists to generate enquiries from businesses looking for a dependable workforce partner. Success means a potential client understands within seconds what Indo does, which industries it serves, that it has years of experience and 10+ clients, and how to get in touch, and then contacts Indo.

## Positioning

**Updated 2026-11-02 (owner brief):** INDO — Frozen Food Workforce Solutions & Industry Expertise. Primary audience is B2B decision-makers in food manufacturing, production, warehousing and logistics (factory/plant, production, operations, supply chain, warehouse, logistics, procurement and HR managers). Frozen food manufacturing leads; production & processing and warehousing & logistics follow; retail is secondary wider experience. Use "workforce" terminology rather than "labour" (labour appears only in the SEO title and the "not just a labour supplier" contrast line). Five supply arrangements: Temporary Workforce Solutions, Temporary-to-Permanent, Seasonal Workforce, Ongoing Workforce Provision, Additional Shift Cover. Four capability areas: Production & Processing, Packing & Quality, Warehouse & Logistics, Supervision & Management. The brand name is written INDO in copy.

Earlier positioning, retained for context:

Indo — People Powering Business.

More than a staffing supplier: an experienced workforce partner that understands the operational demands of high-volume, demanding environments, where having the right people available and well managed keeps production and trading running. Built around three ideas:

1. **Experience:** years of understanding workforce requirements across food production and retail.
2. **People:** finding and supplying the workforce businesses need.
3. **Management:** ongoing workforce coordination and management, not supply alone.

Specialist depth in frozen food manufacturing: Indo has sourced and managed workforces producing frozen pizzas, French fries, frozen fillets and other processed and frozen food products.

Indo is not an HR software provider, payroll platform or technology company, and must never be presented as one.

## Operating Context

Clients run production lines, frozen food plants and retail stores: shift-based, hygiene-controlled, high-volume operations where a missing operative slows a whole line or store. Decision-makers evaluate labour partners on reliability, understanding of the work, and ongoing management support.

## Product Principles

1. Accuracy before persuasion: show only confirmed facts; unverified items stay as visible, editable placeholders.
2. Partner, not supplier: every page shows understanding of the work behind the workforce, not just headcount.
3. Lead with the specialism: frozen food production experience is the most distinctive proof and gets particular attention.
4. Make the enquiry easy, never pushy: a clear route to contact on every page, with honest feedback on what happens to an enquiry.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The brief requires accessible navigation and forms, semantic HTML, a correct heading hierarchy and descriptive alt text. WCAG 2.2 AA is the working standard.
